Junior Data Analyst

We are looking for an entry-level Data Analyst to join our reporting team. Freshers and recent graduates are welcome to apply.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean and organize data from spreadsheets and SQL databases
- Build dashboards and reports using Power BI or Tableau
- Analyze data to find trends and present findings to stakeholders
- Document data sources and reporting steps
- Collaborate with team members to answer business questions

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science or a related field
- 0-2 years of experience in data analysis
- Working knowledge of SQL, Excel and Python (Pandas)
- Strong communication and problem solving skills
- Attention to detail and willingness to learn
