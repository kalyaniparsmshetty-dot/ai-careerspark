Medical Records Assistant

We are hiring an entry-level Medical Records Assistant to support our clinic team.

Responsibilities:
- Maintain accurate clinical documentation and update patient records in the electronic health records (EHR) system
- Schedule appointments and answer patient questions politely
- Follow HIPAA rules to protect patient privacy
- Prepare simple reports for the care team

Requirements:
- Diploma or degree in a health-related subject
- Knowledge of medical terminology
- Strong communication, teamwork and attention to detail
